Your Safety is Our Priority

Deterministic Guardrails

Our safety system uses hard-coded rules, not AI judgment. This means consistent protection against risky financial decisions:

  • ✅ Blocks investments when you have negative cash flow
  • ✅ Prevents large investments if emergency fund is too low
  • ✅ Warns about high-interest debt before investing
  • ✅ Protects short-term goals from risky allocations

Rules before language: Safety checks happen before any recommendation is made. If something isn't safe, we'll explain why and suggest alternatives.
